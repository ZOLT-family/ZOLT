Yes / No markets on Pons V2 launches · chain 4663

Will it graduate?The first launch odds market on Robinhood Chain.

Every few seconds someone launches a token on Pons. Almost every one of them dies on its bonding curve; about one in a hundred reaches the 4.2 ETH that moves it into a real pool. That crossing is a single, objective moment written into Pons’s own contract.

Zolt Odds is a market on that moment. Pick a launch that is still on its curve, stake ETH on yes, it graduates in time or no, it doesn’t, and let the chain settle it. No oracle, no committee, no operator: the outcome is read from the factory that Pons itself trusts.

§ 02

How the odds work

One pool for YES, one for NO. When the market resolves, the losing pool, minus a 1% fee, is shared out to the winning side. Nobody sets a price; the two pools are the price.

mechanism
In order
  1. OpenAnyone opens a market on a Pons launch that is still on its curve, in one of three windows: 10 minutes, 1 hour, 6 hours. One open market per launch per window.
  2. StakeStakes are open for the first half of the window. A stake carries weight equal to its size times the seconds left until staking closes, so money that arrives a second before the close, when the answer is nearly known, earns almost nothing from the pot.
  3. StopThe moment the launch graduates, stakes stop on both sides. The contract reads the factory on every stake.
  4. ResolveYES if the launch left its curve before the deadline; NO if the deadline passed with it still there. Anyone can record either. § 04 has the exact rule.
  5. ClaimWinners take their stake back plus their weighted share of the losing pool. A market with only one side, or one nobody could witness in time, refunds everyone in full.

Graduates at minute 40 → YES. Pot = 1.00 − 1% = 0.99 ETH. A takes 0.10 + 0.99 × 180/186 = 1.058 ETH; C takes 0.10 + 0.99 × 6/186 = 0.132 ETH. C bet the same amount as A on the same side and made a tenth of A’s profit, because C arrived when the curve was almost full.

Implied YES odds on the board = YES pool ÷ (YES + NO). With a 1.4% base rate, a NO pool many times the YES pool is what an honest market looks like; the YES side is small money paid many times over when it lands.

§ 04

How a market resolves

The factory keeps a phase for every launch: NotGraduated on the curve, then Swept the instant a buy crosses the threshold, then PoolCreated when the pool is seeded (or Rescued if it cannot be). Leaving NotGraduated is the event. Only the factory can move it.

no oracle

Three calls, anyone can make them

The market never asks anybody what happened. It asks the factory, and it records who asked first.

witnessYes

Before the deadline, phase ≠ NotGraduated

Any address records YES. Holders of YES have every reason to; the keeper does it anyway.

witnessNo

After the deadline, phase = NotGraduated

Any address records NO. Holders of NO have every reason to; the keeper does it anyway.

voidUnobserved

A day after the deadline, still open

The launch graduated after the deadline before anyone recorded NO. Nobody can prove the order, so nobody wins: every stake is refunded.

Pons’s owner can force a sweep on a launch whose pool cannot be seeded (forceSweptGraduation). That leaves NotGraduated too, and counts as YES: the threshold was reached. It is the one path where a human hand touches the phase, and it is Pons’s hand, not ours.

§ 06

ZOLT

The token has two jobs, both written into the v2 market contract and nowhere else: it halves the fee a winner pays, and it decides which witness is paid for recording an outcome. It promises no yield and pays no dividend. The token has not been launched and the v2 contract is not on mainnet: this section is wired to the tested v2 build and switches on when the deployment record exists.

prepared · not on mainnet yet
Job 1 · the fee discount
  1. BondLock at least 1,000,000 ZOLT in the market contract (planned; fixed at deployment). Every deposit locks the whole bond for seven days.
  2. WinThe fee is charged when a winner claims, on the share of the losing pool they take home, never on their own stake.
  3. Pay halfA bonded winner pays 0.5% of that share instead of 1%. The bond is read at claim, so it cannot be flashed in and out around one.
Job 2 · the witness bounty
  1. BondLock at least 5,000,000 ZOLT (planned; fixed at deployment). Anyone can still record an outcome without one; the bond only decides who is paid for it.
  2. RecordA bonded witness who records YES or NO is paid 0.2% of the losing pool on the spot, before the winners share it.
  3. Nothing to slashOutcomes are read from Pons’s factory, so there is nothing a witness could lie about. Void markets pay nobody.

limits
Final code

The contract has no owner, no pause and no upgrade: what is deployed is what runs. The tests are ours and public. Stake what you can lose in a bug.

Not for everyone

These are event contracts on outcomes. They are not offered to persons in the United States or anywhere else they are not lawful. Nothing here is investment advice.

It reads Pons

If Pons replaces its factory, this contract keeps reading the old one. A new factory means a new market contract.

A creator can buy the answer

Whoever wants a YES can buy the curve to 4.2 ETH. That is not a flaw in the market; it is the thing the market is about. Price it.

Late graduations void

A launch that crosses after the deadline, before anyone recorded NO, refunds both sides. The keeper records NO at the deadline to keep that rare.

Thin pools

A parimutuel pays what the other side staked. A YES against an empty NO refunds; a small NO pool pays a small YES win.

Your wallet, your gas

Every action is a transaction you sign yourself. This page builds it and shows it; it cannot sign for you.

Robinhood Chain can halt

It stopped producing blocks for 14 minutes on 4 September. A halt across a deadline is a halt for everyone; the void rule covers what cannot be witnessed.

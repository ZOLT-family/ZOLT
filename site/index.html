01 · the notice

The schedule goes up first.

An ERC-8056 stock token posts its next multiplier and the second it takes effect. NVDA’s last one went up at block 58,952,659, 588 seconds before it counted. Across all 31 steps, the notice ran 524 to 588 seconds.

02 · the jump

One token becomes four shares.

At that second, every token in every wallet stands for more of the company. Nothing moves. No transfer, no event in the pool. CRWD did exactly this on 2 July: ×4.

03 · the gap

One pool shrugs. One listens.

A pool prices the raw token, so it keeps quoting the old share count. The first buyer gets four shares for the price of one, paid by the LPs. A Stepguard pool read the same notice and prices the step in.

The hook

For any new pool that holds a stock token. Runs before each swap and prices the split into the one direction that would take it. Splits included.

partial protection

The Doppler module

For new Doppler launches that choose it. Runs after each swap, woken by a keeper when a schedule posts, capped at 10% by Doppler: it softens a split, it does not stop one.

the only road to today’s pools

The formula

A small library the authors of the modules already sitting in those slots can put in their next version.

How the guard works

Four steps. Up.

  1. 1

    Register

    When a pool opens, it notes which side is a stock token and the share count its opening price reflects. Pools without one are refused.

  2. 2

    Arm

    Every swap reads uiMultiplier, newUIMultiplier, effectiveAt. When the share count changes, or will soon, it records the price the split implies.

  3. 3

    Charge

    A swap that would take value pays a fee equal to the gap. After a split, buyers pay the new price. The other side pays the normal fee.

  4. 4

    Clear

    Once the pool catches up, crosses the target or the window ends, it goes back to normal. A cancelled schedule clears it too.

What it can’t do

The fine print, in large print.

No pool that exists today

Hooks are fixed at creation and Doppler’s module slots are frozen. The $50.3M already in pools stays exposed.

The Doppler module only softens

A 10% cap, and it hears about a swap after it happens. Without a keeper, the first trade after a split gets through.

It can’t move the price

It charges the gap; the pool catches up as trades arrive. A pool nobody trades is exposed again after the guard window.

The target is fixed when armed

If the market moves while a split is guarded, the fee can be larger or smaller than the true gap.

Its own LPs only

A trader can still take a split from any pool without it.

It fails open

If a token stops answering the ERC-8056 reads, for example after an issuer upgrade, the pool trades at the normal fee.

Unaudited, not deployed

Do not put liquidity behind it until an independent audit.

full protection

The hook

For any new pool holding a stock token. Runs before each swap and prices the split into the one direction that would take value.

partial protection

The Doppler module

For new Doppler launches that choose it. Runs after the swap, woken by a keeper, capped at 10%: it softens a split, it doesn’t stop one.

the only road to today’s pools

The formula

A small library the authors of the modules already sitting in those slots can add to their next version.

how the guard works

Four steps. Up.

  1. 1

    Register

    A new pool notes which side is a stock token, and the share count its price reflects.

  2. 2

    Arm

    Every swap reads uiMultiplier, newUIMultiplier and effectiveAt, and records the price the split implies.

  3. 3

    Charge

    A swap that would take value pays a fee equal to the gap. The other direction pays the normal fee.

  4. 4

    Clear

    Once the pool catches up, crosses the target, or the window ends, it goes back to normal.

No pool that exists today

Hooks are fixed at creation and Doppler’s module slots are frozen. The {{usdExposed}} already sitting in pools stays exposed.

The module only softens

A 10% cap, and it hears about a swap after it happens. Without a keeper, the first trade after a split gets through.

It can’t move the price

It charges the gap; the pool catches up as trades arrive. A pool nobody trades is exposed again once the guard window ends.

The target is fixed when armed

If the market moves while a split is guarded, the fee can be larger or smaller than the true gap.

It fails open

If a token stops answering the ERC-8056 reads, for example after an issuer upgrade, the pool trades at the normal fee.

Unaudited, not deployed, not advice

Do not put liquidity behind it until an independent audit. Stock tokens on Robinhood Chain are tokenised debt securities issued by Robinhood Assets (Jersey) Ltd.

Full protection

The hook

For any new pool that holds a stock token. Runs before each swap and prices the split into the one direction that would take it. Splits included.

Partial protection

The Doppler module

For new Doppler launches that choose it. Runs after each swap, a keeper wakes it when a schedule is posted, and Doppler caps it at 10%, so it only takes the edge off a split.

The only road to today’s pools

The formula

A small library the authors of the modules already sitting in those slots can add to their next version.

How the guard works

Four steps, up.

  1. i

    Register

    When a pool opens, it records which side is a stock token and the share count its opening price reflects. Pools without one are refused.

  2. ii

    Arm

    Every swap reads uiMultiplier, newUIMultiplier and effectiveAt. When the share count changes, or will within the lookahead, it records the price the split implies.

  3. iii

    Charge

    A swap in the direction that would take value pays a fee equal to the gap. After a split, buyers pay the new price. The other side pays the normal fee.

  4. iv

    Clear

    Once the pool trades within the normal fee of the target, crosses it, or the window ends, the pool goes back to normal. A cancelled schedule clears it too.

What it can’t do

Said up front.

  • It protects no pool that exists today.Hooks are fixed at creation and Doppler’s module slots are frozen. The {{usdExposed}} already in pools stays exposed.
  • The Doppler module only softens a split.A 10% cap, and it hears about a swap after it happens. Without a keeper, the first trade after a split gets through.
  • It can’t move the price.It charges the gap; the pool catches up as trades arrive. A pool nobody trades is exposed again after the guard window.
  • The target is fixed when armed.If the market moves while a split is guarded, the fee can be larger or smaller than the true gap.
  • It protects its own LPs.A trader can still take a split from any pool without it.
  • It fails open.If a token stops answering the ERC-8056 reads, for example after an issuer upgrade, the pool trades at the normal fee.
  • Unaudited, not deployed.Do not put liquidity behind it until an independent audit.
